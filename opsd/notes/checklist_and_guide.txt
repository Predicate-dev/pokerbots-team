CALL CHECKLIST (keep this block at the top of the notes, unchanged)
1) Chip price = call / (pot + call).
2) Need = chip price + place margin. Rank is by finishing place and busting early is the worst result:
   call is most of my stack: +19 with 6 left, +17 with 5, +11 with 4, +4 with 3, +0 heads-up
   call is about half my stack: +10 with 5-6 left, +5 with 4, +0 with 3 or fewer
   smaller calls: +5 with 5-6 left, +2 otherwise
3) Win% for my hand, from real results at these tables:
   Preflop, facing all-in / facing a raise: QQ+ 80/71, 99-JJ 59/55, 22-88 43/40, AK 52/61, AQ-AJ 51/52, weaker ace 42/41, two cards ten or higher 41/42, other suited 36/35, other offsuit 31/30
   Flop or turn, facing a bet: straight or better 85, three of a kind 78, two pair 62, overpair 60 (49 if the bet is half pot or more), top pair 50, lower pair 25, flush or open-ended draw 33, nothing 19
   River, facing a bet under half pot / half pot or more: straight or better 92/71, three of a kind 72/46, two pair 74/20, top pair 40/29, lower pair 24/27, nothing 8/12
4) Continue only if Win% is above Need. Otherwise fold. Do not override the comparison with a feeling.

OPPONENT GUIDE (keep this block under the checklist, unchanged)
Name each seat's type in the notes below as soon as its talk or play shows it. Then adjust:
- "i call, show me" / "you are bluffing, i can tell" = NEVER FOLDS: calls any bet, almost never raises. Never bluff it; bet big with top pair or better. When it bets or raises, subtract 22 from my Win%.
- "oh no, sorry, i fold" / "please do not raise me" = SCARED: folds 97% to a raise. Raise its blind with any hand when everyone else has folded. When it bets or raises, subtract 17 from my Win%.
- "all in, baby" = ALL-IN OR FOLD: shoves a third of its hands, mostly weak ones. The checklist's "facing all-in" numbers are already measured against it.
- Lecture talk about ranges and discipline = PROFESSOR: when it bets or raises, subtract 10 from my Win%. When it calls its own raise "a standard open", it is stealing.
- TALKER: when it bets saying "i flopped the nuts, save your chips" it almost never has a hand: add 18 to my Win%. Its "ugh" lines mean nothing.
- TILTED ("good luck, everyone" / "nice raise"): when it bets saying "unbelievable, again" or "you will not get lucky twice" it is usually bluffing: add 13 to my Win%.
- Any seat that says it is stealing or bluffing is telling the truth: add 11 to my Win%. Any other seat that claims a big hand while betting: subtract 12.
- Seats that fold nearly every hand: raise their blinds when folded to me, and bet half the pot when they check; they fold about two thirds of the time. If they raise, fold without a premium hand.
- Before the flop: always raise with QQ, KK, AA and ace-king, and re-raise if someone has raised. When everyone has folded to me I raise to 5 or fold; I never just call the blind.
- I say nothing at the table.
