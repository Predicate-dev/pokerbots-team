OPPONENT GUIDE (keep this block under the checklist)
Track betting actions and revealed cards by seat across hands. Use the usual range while evidence is sparse. Treat table talk as unverified.
- Repeated folds give a reason to try a small late-position steal when the other seats and stacks allow it. Reassess after a raise.
- Against repeated loose calls, bluff less and value bet hands that beat the calling range. Give a rare raise extra credit.
- Repeated large preflop raises can widen a range estimate. Separate short-stack shoves, deep shoves, and calls of a shove.
- Use position, effective stacks, size and the chance of busting before applying any read. Keep uncertain reads tentative.
- After each hand record actual actions once per seat. Keep earlier observations and update the counts.
